    What am I looking at?

    HV is L-band radar sent horizontally and received vertically. Most of it comes from branches and trunks, so it tracks how much forest is standing. Fire removes canopy, and HV drops. Radar sees through smoke and cloud.

    Control is an unburned ring 1 to 3 km outside the fire. It shows changes from season, rain and soil moisture alone. The gap between a burn zone and Control is the fire signal.

    dNBR is the drop in the Sentinel-2 Normalized Burn Ratio. It is the standard optical burn-severity index and the ground truth for the radar.
